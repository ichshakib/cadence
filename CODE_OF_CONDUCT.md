# Code of Conduct

The **Cadence — YouTube Transcript & Bilingual Subtitles** community is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of technical experience.

By participating in this project in any capacity—whether reporting an issue, contributing code, submitting documentation, or participating in discussions—you agree to abide by the principles outlined in this Code of Conduct.

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, respectful, and encouraging community, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in **Cadence** a safe and enriching experience for all individuals, regardless of:

- Age, physical appearance, or body size
- Visible or invisible disabilities and neurodiversity
- Ethnicity, nationality, race, or caste
- Gender identity, expression, and sex characteristics
- Sexual orientation
- Level of experience, education, or socio-economic status
- Religion, philosophical beliefs, or lack thereof

We are committed to building a cooperative, empathetic, and constructive development culture.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a thriving and collaborative community include:

- **Demonstrating Empathy:** Treating every contributor and user with patience, kindness, and respect.
- **Using Inclusive Language:** Choosing language that is welcoming, professional, and non-exclusionary.
- **Constructive Engagement:** Welcoming differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback gracefully, and offering well-reasoned, actionable critiques.
- **Community-First Mindset:** Prioritizing the overall health, performance, and accessibility of the project over individual preferences.
- **Mentorship & Guidance:** Supporting new contributors and answering questions with clarity and patience.

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited within any project space:

- **Harassment & Abuse:** Offensive comments, derogatory jokes, trolling, personal insults, or political attacks.
- **Sexualized Behavior:** The use of sexualized language, imagery, or unwarranted sexual advances.
- **Doxxing & Privacy Violations:** Publishing another person's private details (real name, physical address, email, phone number) without explicit permission.
- **Sustained Disruption:** Repeatedly derailing issues, pull requests, or discussions with off-topic or hostile commentary.
- **Advocating Harm:** Encouraging or condoning harm towards any individual or group.
- **Unprofessional Conduct:** Any action that would be deemed inappropriate or harmful in a professional open-source environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces directly associated with the **Cadence** project, including:

- The GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, comments, wiki, releases)
- GitHub Discussions
- Any official communication channels, chats, or forums associated with Cadence
- Situations where an individual officially represents Cadence in public spaces (e.g., conferences, social media, community meetups)

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers will take fair, prompt, and appropriate corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting & Enforcement

### How to Report

If you experience, witness, or are informed of unacceptable behavior, please report the incident directly to the project maintainer:

- **Maintainer:** Shakib Khan ([@ichshakib](https://github.com/ichshakib))
- **Email:** [ichshakib@gmail.com](mailto:ichshakib@gmail.com)
- **GitHub Profile:** [`https://github.com/ichshakib`](https://github.com/ichshakib)

When submitting a report, please include as much context as possible (description, dates, links, screenshots). All reports will be reviewed promptly and investigated with strict confidentiality.

### Investigation & Privacy

Maintainers will keep the identity of the reporter confidential. Maintainers who have a conflict of interest in a reported incident must recuse themselves from the investigation and enforcement decision.

### Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining consequences for violations:

#### 1. Correction
- **Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.
- **Consequence:** A private, written warning from maintainers, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

#### 2. Warning
- **Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.
- **Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

#### 3. Temporary Ban
- **Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.
- **Consequence:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period.

#### 4. Permanent Ban
- **Community Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.
- **Consequence:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project community.
