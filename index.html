Features

Clean, Focused Subtitle Tools

Designed to be unobtrusive, reliable, and lightweight.

On-Video Player Subtitles

Display synchronized bilingual subtitles directly on top of the YouTube video player in normal, theater, and fullscreen modes.

Synchronized Dual Rows

Follow original spoken lines and their translations stacked in synchronized dual rows with automatic scrolling.

SRT, VTT & Text Import

Upload .srt, .vtt, .txt, or .json files, or paste any transcript text directly into the panel.

Interactive Playback Seeking

Click any dialogue timestamp to jump video playback directly to that moment with zero lag.

Instant Full-Text Search

Search across both original and translated lines simultaneously with real-time keyword highlighting.

20+ Languages

Translate uploaded or pasted subtitles into Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, and more.

Privacy First

No telemetry, tracking, or accounts. Transcripts and language preferences stay entirely in your browser.

Workflow

How It Works

Three simple steps to bilingual subtitles.

1

Open Video

Go to any YouTube video and click the Transcript button next to Like/Dislike.

2

Upload or Paste

Upload your .srt, .vtt, or .txt file, or paste your transcript lines.

3

Enjoy Subtitles

Watch bilingual subtitles in the panel or directly on the video player with one-click toggles.
