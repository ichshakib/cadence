Powerful Features

Engineered for Seamless Learning

Everything you need to study, translate, research, and follow videos with confidence.

Native YouTube Button

Cadence injects a dedicated "Transcript" button right into the YouTube action bar between Like/Dislike and Share for instant 1-click access.

Bilingual Dual Subtitles

Follow the speaker's exact original words alongside your preferred translation language in clear, stacked, synchronized dual rows.

Live Playback Seeking

Every sentence features an interactive timestamp. Click any line to jump the video directly to that scene with smooth auto-scroll.

Lightning Fast Search

Search across both original spoken lines and translated text simultaneously with highlighted matching terms to find exact quotes in seconds.

SRT, VTT & Text Import

Video has no closed captions? Drag & drop your own SRT, WebVTT, JSON, or paste custom text directly into Cadence's universal parser.

Adaptive Theme Match

Cadence detects YouTube's theme changes in real time and automatically adapts between dark and light modes for seamless aesthetics.
